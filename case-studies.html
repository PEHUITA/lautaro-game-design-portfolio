CASE STUDY / 01

Deuda de Sangre

Strategic card game about judging souls, guilt and redemption.

Overview

The player is an ex-servant of a spiritual court, condemned to work at a bridge toll for 1,000 years. Each arriving soul must be evaluated through its dossier, actions and earthly attachments before deciding whether it deserves to reach Heaven.

My Role

Game Design — systems, card mechanics, decision-making structure and player experience.

Design Intent

Create a strategic moral decision game where the player is constantly balancing information, resources and uncertainty rather than simply choosing between good and evil.

Core Mechanics

Impact Weight, Memories vs. Burden, Status, Main Sin, Earthly Anchor, Guilt, Violence, Sacrifice, Soul Mana and Burden Points.

Gameplay Loop

Receive a soul → inspect its dossier → evaluate its actions and attributes → play cards → manage corruption and resources → resolve the soul → continue with the next case.

Key Design Decisions

The system avoids a simple morality meter. Souls are evaluated through several overlapping variables so that each case becomes a small strategic puzzle.

Development Status

Work in progress.

CASE STUDY / 02

Keep It Alive

8-bit card game about fighting a system that is already working against you.

Overview

The player is not a doctor. The game focuses on a personal struggle against an overwhelming healthcare system, represented through a patient, a relative and a series of increasingly oppressive encounters.

My Role

Game Design — card systems, resources, encounter structure, bosses and gameplay loop.

Design Intent

Turn bureaucratic, emotional and medical pressure into gameplay without turning the player into a traditional combat character.

Core Mechanics

Health, Energy / Action Points, Lucid Fatigue, Mild Stress and Exhaustion. Cards are divided into medical, bureaucratic and emotional actions.

Gameplay Loop

Enter an encounter → evaluate the situation → spend energy and cards → react to the boss effect → manage exhaustion → survive the encounter.

Key Design Decisions

Bosses are personifications of illnesses rather than conventional enemies. Fever can burn cards, while Depression can soak cards with tears and temporarily disable them.

Development Status

Work in progress / Unity prototype.

CASE STUDY / 03

Junkborn — Eco de Chatarra

Top-down 2D exploration built around modular scrap equipment.

Overview

The player controls a robot navigating hostile environments while collecting discarded parts. Equipment is temporary: arms, legs and head components degrade and force the player to constantly rethink their build.

My Role

Game Design — equipment systems, progression, resource design and gameplay structure.

Design Intent

Make every piece of junk feel useful while turning equipment degradation into a source of strategic decisions instead of simple punishment.

Core Mechanics

Scrap collection, special rooms, enemy drops, degrading body parts and equipment synergies.

Gameplay Loop

Explore → fight → collect scrap → replace or combine parts → adapt the build → continue exploring.

Key Design Decisions

The equipment system is represented directly on the character's body, with a battery-like UI showing the condition of each component.

Development Status

Work in progress.

CASE STUDY / 04

Último Aliento — Oxígeno Zero

A 2D survival concept where every action has a cost in oxygen.

Overview

The player is trapped in an environment where oxygen is the most important resource. Movement and decisions are tied directly to survival, creating pressure through limited time and limited breathing room.

My Role

Game Design — survival rules, resource pressure and turn-based decision structure.

Design Intent

Make oxygen more than a health bar: it becomes the central resource that determines what the player can afford to do.

Core Mechanics

Turn-based movement, oxygen consumption and survival management.

Gameplay Loop

Assess the situation → choose an action → consume oxygen → react to the new state → decide whether to keep moving or conserve resources.

Key Design Decisions

The design creates tension by making ordinary movement meaningful. Every action pushes the player closer to the limit.

Development Status

Concept / work in progress.

CASE STUDY / 05

Grey Market — El Gremio del Espectro

A strategic underground economy built around information, reputation and influence.

Overview

The player operates inside a modern underground network where information and influence are more valuable than direct force. The presentation combines suited characters, exaggerated expressions and a comic-inspired visual identity.

My Role

Game Design — strategic structure, dilemmas, progression concepts and player decision-making.

Design Intent

Transform the logic of an underground information network into a strategic game where decisions affect reputation, influence and future opportunities.

Core Mechanics

Map-based progression, dilemmas, information management, reputation and influence.

Gameplay Loop

Identify an opportunity → evaluate the dilemma → choose an action → manage the consequences → expand influence.

Key Design Decisions

The structure takes inspiration from the readability of map-based strategy games while focusing the player's attention on decisions and consequences rather than combat.

Development Status

Concept / work in progress.

CASE STUDY / 06

Desfase — Shift Protocol

A 3D concept built around physical-environment desynchronization and inertia.

Overview

Shift Protocol explores a world where the relationship between the player and the physical environment becomes unreliable. The core idea revolves around desynchronization, spatial perception and physical inertia.

My Role

Game Design — core concept, interaction ideas and experimental gameplay direction.

Design Intent

Explore how changing the rules of physical interaction can become the main source of gameplay rather than simply serving as a visual effect.

Core Mechanics

Environmental desynchronization, altered physical relationships and inertia-based interaction concepts.

Gameplay Loop

Observe the environment → identify the physical inconsistency → interact with it → adapt to the new state → progress.

Key Design Decisions

The concept prioritizes experimentation with physical rules and player perception over traditional genre conventions.

Development Status

Concept / work in progress.
